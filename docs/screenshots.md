## Evidence checklist

- Staff can create and manage meetings.
- Guests can be imported and invited.
- Participants receive a link-based RSVP and attendance experience.
- WhatsApp delivery can be tested through the configured Meta integration.
- The application provides administration, delivery status, attendance, and reporting views.
